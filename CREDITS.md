CC0 verified from the License.txt inside each downloaded zip (2026-11-14); the Farm Animals pack has no license file, its itch page states CC0 (checked 2026-11-15). The cow, pig and horse in `art/town/farm/animals` are converted from it (scale, heading, animation names) by `tools_src/convert_farm_animals.py`. Characters in `art/characters` are assembled from these packs by `tools_src/build_characters.py`. The stone figures (cathedral saints, fountain water bearers) are those same CC0 characters posed and dressed by `tools_src/build_cathedral.py` / `build_plaza_props.py`. Ground, coreto, fountains, palms, shop dressings and the cathedral are modelled from primitives by the scripts in `tools_src/` (original work, MIT like the rest of the code); their textures are generated or tinted from the Medieval Village MegaKit textures. The farm (farmhouse, barn, yard, fields, crops, wagons in `art/town/farm`) is modelled from primitives by `tools_src/build_farm.py`. The Mula sem cabeça (`art/mobs/mula_sem_cabeca`, Brazilian folklore, public domain) uses the skeleton and animations of the Farm Animals horse; its body is adapted from "Equine Model" by Lova Dahn (CC BY 4.0, licence checked on the model page 2026-11-16; changes: head removed at mid-neck, mesh decimated, neck raised and thickened, rigged, retextured) by `tools_src/mula_base.py`; its veins, chains, flames and textures are generated by `tools_src/build_mula_sem_cabeca.py` and `tools_src/mula_body.py` (original work).
